# San4 Owner Landing Pages: Draft Copy

Four pages, one template. Each page follows the same order so one React component can render all of them from a data object:

1. Hero (headline, subhead, two CTAs)
2. The problem (3 pains in the owner's own words)
3. How San4 helps (3 steps)
4. What you get (feature cards, each with a status tag)
5. What your learners do (the learner's side)
6. Proof (real content only)
7. Offer / pricing note
8. FAQ
9. Final CTA

## Status tags (important)

Every feature card carries one tag so the page never promises what isn't built:

- **LIVE**: works in the product today.
- **PILOT**: delivered by hand to early owners while it's being built.
- **SOON**: on the roadmap, not yet available.

## Facts used, from the product

- The AI coach is **Vak**. Learners practise scenarios, get scored on pace, filler words, confidence and structure, and get one clear fix per session.
- Learners get a **San4 Score** and a shareable score card.
- Plans: **Base Camp ₹0** (levels 1 to 10 earned by scoring), **Vak Pro ₹299/month** (introductory price, was ₹499), **Vak Elite ₹999/month**. Pro includes camera-on Body Language coaching, regional-language support and all 14 levels. Elite includes the Call Analyzer for real Zoom/Meet/Teams recordings.
- Hindi, Marathi, Telugu and other regional language support exists, plus mother-tongue onboarding.
- Contact: `aman@san4.in`.
